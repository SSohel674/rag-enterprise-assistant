# Acme Corp IT Security Policy

## Passwords
Passwords must be at least 12 characters and include upper and lower case letters, numbers and a symbol. Passwords expire every 90 days and the last 5 passwords cannot be reused. Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for all corporate accounts.

## Device Usage
Company laptops must have full-disk encryption and automatic screen lock after 5 minutes of inactivity. Personal devices may access corporate email only through the managed Mobile Device Management (MDM) app.

## Incident Reporting
Any suspected security incident, such as phishing, lost devices or unauthorised access, must be reported to security@acme.example within 1 hour of discovery. Do not delete suspicious emails; forward them as attachments.

## Data Classification
Data is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential or Restricted. Confidential and Restricted data must never be stored on personal cloud storage or sent over unencrypted channels.

## Software Installation
Only software from the approved catalogue may be installed. Requests for new software go through the IT Service Desk and take up to 3 working days.
