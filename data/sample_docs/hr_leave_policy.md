# Acme Corp HR Leave Policy

## Annual Leave
Full-time employees are entitled to 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave accrues monthly at 2 days per month. Up to 10 unused days can be carried forward to the next year; any remaining balance lapses on 31 March.

## Sick Leave
Employees receive 12 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for absences longer than 2 consecutive days. Sick leave cannot be carried forward or encashed.

## Maternity and Paternity Leave
Maternity leave is 26 weeks of fully paid leave. Paternity leave is 10 working days, which must be taken within 3 months of the child's birth.

## Work From Home
Employees may work from home up to 2 days per week with manager approval. Fully remote arrangements need written approval from the department head and HR.

## Applying for Leave
Leave requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least 5 working days in advance, except for sick leave and emergencies. Managers must approve or reject requests within 2 working days.
